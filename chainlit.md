# Al-Noor Electronics · Shop Desk

Ask what's in stock, what it costs, or order a few items. Prices come only from the shop's catalogue.

- **Chat profile:** *Walk-in* or *Regular* customer. Regular customers are offered the 5% loyalty price.
- **Settings (gear icon):** set a simulated hour to see how delivery promises change outside shop hours.
- Under each reply, the **Cost** step shows that turn's model calls, tokens and models, plus a link to the conversation's trace.
